Deadlock Handling Theory

In modern operating systems, handling deadlocks follows four primary philosophies: Ignore (Ostrich Algorithm), Detection & Recovery, Dynamic Avoidance (Banker's), or Structural Prevention by breaking one of the Coffman conditions.
